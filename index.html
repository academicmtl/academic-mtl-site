How it works

01 — Design

You bring the vision

Submit a graduate or independent collection. We look for point of view first — polish, we can help with.

02 — Produce

We handle manufacturing

Sampling, fit approval, and small-batch production through our Montreal-area manufacturing partners — on your name, at your standard.

03 — Platform & Sell

We get it in front of people

Your collection lives on our site and comes to life at our pop-ups. You get paid on what sells, with full visibility into the numbers.
